# Italy outcomes — ISTAT 2025

**Status: extract present.** Retrieved 2026-08-15 from the official ISTAT release.

## What this folder is

Hand-copied cells from ISTAT’s fourth survey on the professional placement of
doctorate holders (2025 interview, 2019 and 2021 cohorts — **four and six years**
after the degree).

There is **no mathematics-only row**. The closest official group is
**Scientifico**. ISTAT footnote iii of the report:

> Il gruppo scientifico include Biologia, Chimica, Biotecnologie, Scienze della
> nutrizione, Matematica, Statistica, Fisica.

On this site that group is labeled **Scientifico**, never “mathematics PhDs.”

Sector rows (university, non-university education, research institutes) are
printed **for all Italian PhDs**, not for Scientifico. They stay in a separate
block.
